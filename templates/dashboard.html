How these numbers are calculated
  • Sales (revenue) is POS sales only, after discounts. Website orders have no stored price (a reorder intentionally re-prices at today's rate), so online orders are shown as a count, never blended into a Rs figure.
  • Cash collected = non-credit payment portions of POS sales + credit repayments received in the period. This is not the same as revenue — a credit sale is revenue immediately but cash only when repaid.
  • Credit given = the credit portion of POS sales in the period. Outstanding credit is a live snapshot (all-time), matching the Credit tab exactly — it isn't scoped to the filter date range.
  • Per-product / per-category revenue is an estimate ("est."), priced at today's rates — individual sale lines don't store historical prices. Quantity/weight breakdowns are exact.
  • Offer discounts are not tracked as a Rs amount (only that an offer was used); coupon discounts are exact (stored on the sale).
  • No profit/margin figures anywhere — products have no recorded cost price.
  • Dates use Nepal local time (Asia/Kathmandu). Dismissed/failed offline POS sales are never saved to the database (only a Telegram alert) — this dashboard reflects synced sales only.
